Berlinan FM-1

The generative Berlin School sequencer, as firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. Short melodic loops repeat, slowly shift against each other and change only a little at a time, and you play the ensemble from the FM-1's keys, buttons and knobs. No laptop needed.

It follows the rules of the genre

The engine is Berlinan's: a style manual compiled from about 225 video lessons by a Berlin School practitioner, turned into four laws that do most of the work.

Repetition earns change
A pattern plays verbatim several times before it may mutate, and every mutation has to earn its place again. The dots on the Perform page count the plays left.
One scale for everyone
All voices share one scale. The white keys move the whole ensemble by scale degree, so it changes harmony together and never leaves the key.
Phasing, not randomness
Voices run loops of different lengths and drift against each other. The screen's LCM says how many bars until they line up again.
Change on the bar
What you press lands on the next bar line; a voice's new pattern waits for the end of its own loop. Press a little early and it still arrives in time.

It plays exactly what Berlinan for Mac plays

The engine is Berlinan for macOS's, translated into C. The same template, the same seed and the same actions at the same bars give the same notes and the same CC, event for event, tick for tick: the tests check it against the unchanged Swift engine. The Setup page shows the seed, so a run on the FM-1 can be repeated on the Mac.

Berlinan for macOS, the app it comes from, adds harmony modes, melody extraction, Ableton Link, capture and MIDI export.

Three templates to start from

Turn PRESETS to pick one; while playing it takes over at the next bar. Each starts in A minor from a fresh seed. ALGORITHM picks the mode, the Setup page the root, and the Voice page moves any voice to another channel.

It plays your synths

The FM-1 makes no sound of its own. Connect it by USB to a MIDI router (a USB MIDI host box, or a computer that passes MIDI on); it shows up as FM-Berlinan. Each voice goes out on its own MIDI channel, so every synth plays its part.

INT The FM-1 is the clock master: MIDI Start, clock and Stop over USB.

EXT It follows MIDI clock from USB or the TRS jack, and holds the tempo if the clock drops out.

SAVE The project and six snapshot slots stay through power-off.

Made for playing live

Press PLAY and the ensemble starts at once. From there, every move is one press and lands on the next bar.

  • Voice keysTap to mute, hold to select. A voice the arranger holds back comes in with a tap.
  • Degree keysThe white keys move the whole ensemble to degrees 1 to 7.
  • The B partREC: a contrasting second section in one press. With a voice key held: a blank cycle for that voice.
  • Arranger autopilotBuilds the piece voice by voice, varies it, brings the theme back and ends it. >> moves it on.
  • The density faderMASTER thins the ensemble out or fills it, from 0 to 100 %.
  • Scenes and tensionThe black keys morph your synths' controllers to the template's scenes over 8 bars; G5 adds a moment of tension.
  • Snapshots, Dice and undoSix slots recalled on the bar, a new seed on OCT+, 20 steps of undo on OCT-.
  • Help on every buttonHold any button for a second and a card says what it does. Let go and nothing happens.

Get Berlinan for the FM-1

Install from the browser

Open the install page in Chrome or Edge, connect the FM-1 by USB and press Install; allow MIDI when the browser asks. The page checks the firmware before it writes, and the FM-1 reports its version when it restarts. Nothing is installed on the computer.

Install version 0.5.1

Installing firmware is at your own risk. If the FM-1 won't start, a Mac can put the stock firmware back.

Back to M-VAVE's firmware

The same page puts M-VAVE's own firmware (FM-1 V15) back: download the file from M-VAVE, choose it on the page and press the button. The page checks it is M-VAVE's before it writes. M-VAVE's own updater, M-UPGRADE, does it too.

Back to the stock firmware · m-vave.com/download

From the command line

Download berlinan-0.5.1.fwsc, then, with fm1-base's installer from the source:

pip3 install mido python-rtmidi
python3 base/tools/fm1_install.py berlinan-0.5.1.fwsc

Build from source

Free software under the GPL 2.0 or later. The build needs Docker (or podman) and JieLi's toolchain; BUILDING.md has the details. 

git clone --recursive https://github.com/jiripisa/fm1-berlinan.git
cd fm1-berlinan
./build.sh
